Judges & the TKEthics partnership

Matches are judged by professional ethicists and experienced debate coaches sourced through our partnership with TKEthics and the Modus Ponens Institute, following the official Ethics Olympiad scoring criteria.

How each case is scored

  • Initial presentation15
  • Response to commentary15
  • Response to judges' questions15
  • Commentary on the other team's case10
  • Respectful dialogue, not combative debate5

60 points per case, per team. Read the full rubric or browse prep resources.
