## Overall Impression

Type and color tokens (four-family dark-academia system, indigo/teal palette) are more ambitious than what's on screen. Layout never grew into them: every section is the same 920px centered column, only teal ever surfaces as an accent, and there is zero imagery anywhere on a page recruiting teenagers to a live event.

## What's Working

1. Copy voice — specific, confident, not filler ("Forget everything you know about debate," detailed FAQ).
2. Token foundation — Cinzel + Playfair + Cormorant + Inter, indigo→teal gradient, considered starting palette, underused not badly chosen.
3. .format__step-number oversized Cinzel numeral breaks from gray-card monotony — worth extending.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Every section has identical shape and rhythm
- Why it matters: by section 6 of 9, a scrolling visitor has seen the same beat five times and starts skimming.
- Fix: alternate section background tone; widen 1-2 sections past the narrow 920px column; break at least one section into an asymmetric 2-column layout.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout

[P1] No imagery anywhere on a live-event page
- Why it matters: only visuals are two 32px header logos; hero and all sections are text-in-boxes, undersells event credibility/energy.
- Fix: hero background treatment (line motif, animated gradient mesh, ethics/dialogue visual); replace a "coming soon" placeholder with real graphic.
- Suggested command: /impeccable delight

[P1] One accent color used for everything
- Why it matters: teal marks CTA, dates, step numbers, and card borders identically (confirmed by ai-color-palette x13); indigo never surfaces standalone.
- Fix: reserve teal for interactive/primary elements; let indigo appear standalone somewhere; desaturate metadata instead of teal-coding it.
- Suggested command: /impeccable colorize

[P1] Type system loaded but not used — Inter is 73% of all text
- Why it matters: Playfair/Cormorant appear once each; dark-academia identity never materializes past the hero; h3 inconsistently Cinzel vs Playfair by section.
- Fix: give 2-3 more sections a deliberate serif/display moment (headers, pull-quotes, FAQ answers).
- Suggested command: /impeccable typeset

[P2] No :focus-visible styling anywhere; mobile nav wraps instead of collapsing
- Why it matters: inconsistent keyboard focus outlines on dark background; 390px viewport nav wraps to two cramped rows.
- Fix: explicit :focus-visible style using accent tokens; mobile nav breakpoint with collapsed/hamburger pattern.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt
